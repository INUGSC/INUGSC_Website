2011 to 2026

Past conferences

Fifteen conferences since UCL Surgical Society founded INUGSC in 2011. The 16th is on 30 and 31 January 2027, at UCL East.

  1. 2026

    The 15th conference

    Innovate, Initiate, Interrelate

    Keynote from Prof Owase Jeelani, with Mr Ashley Simpson, Prof Jayant Vaidya and Mr Ioannis Loukopoulos. 19 hands-on workshops. 95% of in-person delegates would recommend it.

  2. 2025

    The 14th conference

    Surgeons of Tomorrow

    With Prof Anna David, Prof Jag Dhanda and Dr Azad Najar. Delegates from ten countries.

  3. 2024

    The 13th conference

    Surgery: For All, To All?

    With Prof John Kelly and Mr Amer Shoaib.

  4. 2023

    The 12th conference

    Surgery Against All Odds

    UCL Surgical Society received UCL's Opportunities Award, after INUGSC was recognised as the largest student-led surgical conference in the UK.

  5. 2022

    The 11th conference

    Navigating Surgical Evolution

    With Prof Ava Kwong and Prof Anthony Atala. More than 500 delegates, and the first widening participation programme.

  6. 2021

    The 10th anniversary

    Changing the Paradigm of Surgery

    Held online.

  7. 2020

    The 9th conference

    One day, 25 January, with a keynote from Prof Karl Schaller. Named UCL Event of the Year.

  8. 2018

    International

    The conference opens to delegates from abroad and becomes INUGSC.

  9. 2011

    The first conference

    UCL Surgical Society founds the National Undergraduate Surgical Conference, NUGSC.

Help fill the gaps

We are still piecing together the years between 2011 and 2019 from the archive. If you organised or spoke at an earlier INUGSC, we would love to hear from you.
