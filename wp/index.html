INUGSC 2027

Widening participation

A second stream of the conference for sixth-form students from widening participation backgrounds.

What it is

Widening participation is the second stream of INUGSC. While medical students attend the main conference, sixth formers from widening participation backgrounds follow a programme of their own.

Through talks and hands-on workshops, they explore a career in medicine more deeply, meet people already on that path, and widen their curiosity about what medicine and surgery involve.

Places are free. Many of the students we welcome receive free school meals, and cost should never be the reason someone misses out.

What students do

Suturing
Hands-on practice placing stitches on a skin pad.
Anatomy
Anatomy and dissection models to handle and explore.
Virtual reality
Surgical virtual-reality sets that simulate an operation.
Talks
Talks, and time to ask medical students and doctors about the path into medicine.

Why it matters

  • 5% of medical school entrants come from the lowest socio-economic backgrounds
  • 75% come from higher socio-economic backgrounds
  • 1.5× more likely to get a medicine offer when applying from an independent school rather than a non-selective state school

From the Sutton Trust’s report Unequal Treatment? (2025), based on research at UCL Medical School into UK medical school applicants and entrants from 2012 onwards. The 1.5× difference remains after accounting for exam grades and background.
